# Bob — Story Bible & Shorts Production Workflow

## 1. The animation style (what every Bob video must look like)

| Layer | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Flat, drawn-looking 2D character living inside a 3D world (2.5D). Off-white disc head, thick charcoal outline, charcoal stroke face, ink-line arms, blue robe, yellow sash, striped topknot with gold band. |
| **Face acting** | All the emotion comes from **eyes, brows and a tiny mouth**. The default face is the **deadpan dash**. Big moments switch to **round eyes**; smug or doubtful moments use **half-lid "D" eyes**. Brows sell everything. |
| **Body acting** | Minimal. Hold poses, then one clear gesture: point, raise a finger, shrug, hands up. Snap between poses (2–4 frames), then hold for 8–20 frames. No floaty motion. |
| **World** | Saturated, detailed 3D sets: palaces, castle walls, markets, caves, night roofs. Use the **glassy grade**: bloom, haze, tinted shadows, one warm and one cool light. The background is always a bit out of focus, and Bob is always sharp. |
| **Moods** | `day` (explaining), `palace` (power, money), `sunset` (emotional twist), `night` (mystery, sneaking, danger). |
| **Camera** | Vertical 9:16. Slow push-ins on Bob's face for reactions, quick cut to a wide shot for the world. Small camera shake on impacts. |
| **Captions** | 1–3 words at a time, centred low-middle, white bold with a thick black outline, each word appearing on the beat of the voice, as in the reference frames. |
| **Sound** | Calm narrator voice, light comedic music bed, a sound effect on **every** cut or expression change (pop, whoosh, gulp, boing). Silence for 0.3 s before the punchline. |
| **Length / pace** | 45–60 s, a new shot every **1.5–3 s**, about 140–160 spoken words. |

## 2. The Bob world

- **Setting:** *The Kingdom of Lumen*, a timeless storybook land where every strange true story from
  history "happens to Bob". It mixes palace halls, castle towns, deserts, seas and caves, so any
  real-history story can be staged there.
- **Bob:** a deadpan court clerk who keeps getting assigned the kingdom's weirdest jobs. He never
  complains out loud; his **eyes** say everything. His catchphrase look is the slow half-lid stare
  at the camera.
- **Recurring cast** (Bob variants: same rig, different colours and hats):
  - **The King:** red robe, tall black hat, always smiling, terrible ideas.
  - **The Guard:** helmet, spear, takes everything literally.
  - **The Narrator:** never seen. Speaks to the viewer like a friend telling a secret.

**Story formula for every episode (the "Bob loop"):**
1. **Hook (0–2 s):** a weird image + one shocking line. Never start with context.
2. **Setup (2–12 s):** the strange rule, job or event, told in 3 short facts.
3. **Escalation (12–35 s):** each beat gets weirder; Bob's face reacts to each one.
4. **Twist (35–48 s):** the surprising real outcome.
5. **Question (48–55 s):** ask the viewer something they *must* answer in the comments
   ("Would you…?", "What do you think really happened?").
6. **Loop:** the last shot cuts back to the first image so the video replays seamlessly. Loops raise
   watch time, which the algorithm rewards.

## 3. Episode 1 — "The Man Paid to Never Bathe" (≈55 s)

Based on the popular account of 18th-century English *ornamental hermits*. At Painshill Park,
Charles Hamilton is said to have offered a large fee to a man who would live as a hermit for 7 years
under strict rules; the story goes that the hermit was found in a pub after about three weeks.
Details vary between sources, so present it as "the story goes…".

| # | Time | Shot (vertical) | Bob's face (rig) | Mood | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–2.0 | Close-up: Bob in a dark cave, huge fake beard, staring at camera | `Bored` + look at camera | night | "This man was paid to **never** take a bath… for seven years." |
| 2 | 2–5 | Wide: fancy garden with fountains | `Neutral` | day | "Three hundred years ago, rich lords wanted something weird in their gardens." |
| 3 | 5–8 | Statue wobbles, then Bob pops up beside it | `Surprised` (round eyes) | day | "Not statues. Not fountains… a real, living hermit." |
| 4 | 8–11 | A scroll unrolls on the palace wall | `Thinking` | palace | "One lord posted the job." |
| 5 | 11–17 | Rules appear one by one as icons (no talking, scissors crossed out, hourglass) | `Smirking` → `Confused` | palace | "Live in a cave. Never speak. Never cut your hair, your beard… or your nails." |
| 6 | 17–21 | Gold coins rain, the bloom glows | eyes `lids_wide` + `mouth_grin` | palace | "The prize? A fortune in gold." |
| 7 | 21–24 | Bob signs the contract, quill scratch SFX | `Smirking` | palace | "One man said yes." |
| 8 | 24–30 | Montage: beard grows, sweat drop, spider on his head | `Bored` → `Scared` (sweat) | sunset | "Week one: peaceful. Week two: very… very quiet." |
| 9 | 30–35 | Bob's eyes slide toward a glowing tavern sign far away | `look_x` + half-lid | night | "Week three…" |
| 10 | 35–41 | Tavern: Bob laughing, mug raised, beard in his drink | `Laughing` | night (warm lantern) | "…the story goes, he was found at the local pub." |
| 11 | 41–46 | Guard bursts in; Bob freezes | `Surprised` → `Sad` | night | "He was fired on the spot." |
| 12 | 46–52 | Push-in on Bob staring at the viewer | `Thinking`, slow blink | sunset | "So… would **you** live in a cave for seven years… for a fortune?" |
| 13 | 52–55 | Caption "YES or NO? 👇", then cut back to shot 1 | `Neutral` | night | *(music sting, then loop)* |

**Pinned comment:** "Be honest… how many days would YOU last? 🕳️"

## 5. Production workflow (repeatable, about 1 episode per day)

| Step | Tool | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Research + script** | Wikipedia + 2 sources; write about 150 words in the hook → setup → escalate → twist → question shape | `script.md` |
| 2. **Voice-over** | ElevenLabs text-to-speech (calm narrator voice), or record yourself | `vo.wav` |
| 3. **Timing sheet** | Import the voice-over into Blender's Video Sequencer or DaVinci Resolve; mark a cut on each sentence | shot timings (table like §3) |
| 4. **Sets** | Reuse the Bob sets; extend them with free assets from Poly Haven (textures, HDRIs, models), BlenderKit and Kenney. Apply `glassy_grade.apply_mood()` and `apply_grade()` per shot. | `sets/*.blend` |
| 5. **Layout** | Link `bob_character.blend` into the shot file (File → Link → collection `BOB`), make a library override, and place the camera at 1080×1920. | `shot_##.blend` |
| 6. **Animation** | Key **body poses** with the rig (IK hands for pointing and holding). Key the **face** with the Bob panel (N → Bob → Keyframe on click): set an expression on the beat, hold it, then blink. Use **Constant** or snappy Bezier interpolation for the face. | animated shots |
| 7. **Lip-sync** | Key the visemes `vis_A/E/O/U/MBP/FV` on the stressed syllables only (every 3–4 frames); keep the neutral "o" between words | talking shots |
| 8. **Render** | EEVEE on your GPU at 1080×1920, 30 fps, 64 samples, `bob_scene_tint` 0.5–0.7 | PNG sequences |
| 9. **Edit** | CapCut or DaVinci Resolve: cut to the voice-over, add music, a sound effect on every cut, word-by-word captions, and a 2–3 % zoom-in on reaction shots | `episode.mp4` |
| 10. **Publish** | Title = the hook line. First frame = the hook image. Ask the question again in the caption, and pin a comment. Post the same video to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Reels. | — |

**Rules that keep viewers watching**
- Something must change on screen every 2 seconds: a cut, an expression or a caption.
- The hook image must be strange **without sound**, because many people scroll on mute.
- Never explain the twist early; tease it ("…and then week three happened").
- End on the question, then loop to the first shot.
